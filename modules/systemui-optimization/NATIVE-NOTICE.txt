The bounded ptrace guard is reused from this repository's controller-whitening module.
expected.h, wait_expected.h and schema_expected.h contain instruction signatures from the Samsung-distributed
libImpress-jni-vulkan.so solely for compatibility checking and restoration.
panel_code.h includes small instruction signatures from Samsung-distributed
libspaceflinger_jni_vulkan.so and libsysui_jni_soong_vulkan.so for the same purpose,
plus locally generated ARM64 panel-coalescing code.
Samsung, Google, Impress, Filament, OpenXR and Android retain their attribution.
No complete proprietary library is distributed.
